# AI Policy

There are many issues I have with AI, but in the context of my projects, I do not care if people use AI as long as it is not inflicted on me. Software development is a hobby for me and interacting with humans and human-written content is part of what keeps it fun.

Please avoid dumping a giant blob of AI-generated text in issues and PRs. It's fine to mention, for example, that you used AI to troubleshoot an issue. Just make an effort to explain what the issue is in your own words instead of copying and pasting a bunch of AI-generated output. Things that looks like complete slop or like there was 0 human thought involved may result in a warning or ban.

AI-generated code is not accepted in my projects. If I find the requested changes in a PR interesting enough to consider including, I may ask the author if they are fine with me reimplementing the idea from scratch, with attribution in `Co-Authored-By` if appropriate.
