# AI Policy

There are many issues I have with AI, but in the context of my projects, I do not care if people use AI as long as it is not inflicted on me. I only wish to interact with humans and human-written content. AI ruins any kind of enjoyment I have in software development and interacting with the community.

This is what can be expected for my projects:

* Issues and PRs that (subjectively) appear to contain AI generated content will be closed and may result in a warning or ban, depending on how close it is to AI slop.
    * This does not include people who are asking for help because they followed AI instructions/suggestions that did not work. Just avoid copying copying and pasting some giant blob of AI output.

* Where possible, dependencies containing less AI-generated code will be preferred over dependencies that contain more.

* The software will never integrate with AI tools or services, but will also not actively impede usage with them.
    * For example: The software will not just exit if the user happens to have an AI tool running.

* The software will not be actively malicious towards people who use AI tools.
    * For example: The software will not include prompt injection strings that try to convince AI tools to delete the user's files.
